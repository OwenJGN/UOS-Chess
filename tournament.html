Chess Summer School

Overview

About the Summer School

Open to all players aged 18 and under - from Sussex or beyond, beginner or seasoned. A fun, inclusive, and enriching chess experience with tailored workshops, personalised game analysis, and 1-to-1 coaching. Supported by the ECF, Sussex Junior Chess, and Kingston Chess Club.

Tailored Workshops

Major group — more experienced players:

  • Pawn structures & middlegame plans
  • Opening themes & sacrifices
  • Rook endgames & saving lost positions

Minor group — developing players:

  • Piece development & tactical patterns
  • Basic strategic thinking

'My First Chess Day' — expert-guided introduction for brand new players.

Personalised Experience

Send up to three of your games before the event. Trainers will analyse them and suggest a personalised schedule — though you remain free to choose whichever sessions you like.

Chess Clinic

Choose one game to review 1-to-1 with an experienced trainer. They can also provide a long-term development plan tailored to you.

Trainers & Safeguarding

Sessions run by Sussex University's top players and Sussex Junior Chess coaches. All trainers hold enhanced DBS clearance.

  • Under 8s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian throughout
  • Over 8s may be supervised by a nominated adult (not a coach)
  • Parents/guardians remain responsible for their children at all times
