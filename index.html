About Us

Chess at Sussex

Whether you want to learn the rules, play a few relaxed games after lectures, improve seriously, or meet people who also love chess, you are welcome here.

♟

Weekly Chess

Drop in for casual games, analysis, puzzles and competitive practice. You do not need a rating or tournament experience.

🤝

Socials

Meet people away from the board too. We run society socials and relaxed events throughout the academic year.

🏆

Competitions

Play internal events, team chess and other competitive opportunities when you want something more serious.

Weekly Meet-up

Come along to club night

Every Tuesday during term time

6:00pm – 9:00pm · Room T.B.C.

University of Sussex, Falmer, Brighton. Just turn up and play - bring anyone you want!.

Ask us anything →

New to chess?

Perfect. You do not need to know openings, notation or have a rating. We are happy to help you get started.

Already competitive?

You will find plenty of stronger players for serious games, analysis and preparation alongside the casual side of the society.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. If anything changes about a session, Instagram is the best place to check for the latest society update.

Join Us

Your first club night is easy

There is no trial, rating or experience requirement. Come along on a Tuesday, find us in Room T.B.C. and play as much or as little chess as you like.

👋

Just turn up

You do not need to message us beforehand. Come by yourself or bring a friend - either is completely fine.

♟

All levels welcome

Complete beginner, casual player or experienced tournament player: there will be people to play and learn with.

📍

Tuesdays · Room T.B.C.

We meet from 6:00pm to 9:00pm during term time at the University of Sussex.
