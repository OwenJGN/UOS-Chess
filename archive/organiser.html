Meet the Organiser

From a single weekend congress to a week-long Chess Festival with Grandmasters and norm opportunities - the story of how it all came together.

Hello

Hooman Honarvarmahalati

Hooman is the organiser of the Sussex University Chess Congress - now grown into the full Sussex University Chess Festival, bringing together a rated Congress, the Masters norm tournament, a Blitz, an Arbiter's course, and the Junior Summer School in one week each July.

The Journey

From One Congress to a Festival

2024

1st Congress — The First Congress

The inaugural Sussex University Chess Congress takes place - a single weekend, ECF rated, and the start of everything that followed.

2025

2nd Congress — The Second Congress

The event returns for a second year, with entries growing and the field getting stronger as word spreads around the South East chess scene.

2025

3rd Congress — First IM Norm Event

The Congress adds an IM Norm Round Robin alongside the main weekend event, and the Summer School joins for the first time - competitive chess, education, and professional chess all under one roof.

2026

4th Congress — Weekend Congress Returns

The 4th Sussex University Weekend Congress runs in the spring including a charity event, continuing to grow the standard event ahead of the biggest step yet.

2026

5th Congress — The Festival Arrives

The biggest step yet: a full Sussex University Chess Festival in July - the 5th Congress, the brand-new Masters norm tournament with Grandmasters, the Blitz, an Arbiter's course, and the Summer School, all run across one week.
