General

About the Society & Events

No — all of our tournaments, the Summer School, and club nights are open to everyone, whether you're a Sussex student, from another university, or not a student at all. All abilities are welcome, from complete beginners to titled players.

Entering & Fees

Yes — GMs, WGMs and IMs enter both the Congress and the Masters free of charge. We're delighted to welcome strong titled players and it helps raise the standard of the whole event.

Yes. Student and Under-18 entry to the U2050/U1700 sections is £40 (instead of £48), and U1300 is £30 (instead of £35). See the tournament page for the full fee list.

We strongly recommend entering in advance via the Google Form so we can plan pairings and sections properly. Late and on-the-day entries are sometimes possible — please email us before travelling to check availability.

Please let us know as soon as possible by emailing hellosuchess@gmail.com so we can adjust the draw. Refund policy is considered case by case — get in touch and we'll talk it through.

On the Day

Just yourself, a pen (score sheets are provided), and any refreshments you'd like — though there's also food and drink available on site. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your round.

Pay & display parking is available across the University of Sussex campus on tournament days — sat-nav postcode BN1 9RH. See the parking section for more.

Falmer station is a five-minute walk from the Student Centre, with direct trains from Brighton and Lewes. Buses 25 and 25X also run from Brighton city centre. Full details are on the getting here section.

Masters & Titled Players
